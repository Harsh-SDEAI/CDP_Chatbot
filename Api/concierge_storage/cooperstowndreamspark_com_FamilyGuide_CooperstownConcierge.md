Early Arrival: Recommendations

- Arrive a day early to settle in. Come down to Dreams Park for the fireworks and Championship Game.

Day One: Recommendations

- Enjoy the morning in Cooperstown have lunch, visit Main Street.

Day Two: Recommendations

3:30 PM Game Time

- After the conclusion of your game, would be a wonderful time to organize a team event or to visit the National Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum. Once your game is complete, you would have the rest of the night to have a team event offsite.

5:30 PM Game Time

- Before your game, spend time exploring the shops in the Cooperstown Core on Main Street.

7:30 PM Game Time

- Order lunch to go from a Cooperstown Core restaurant and a have a team picnic at Glimmerglass State Park.

Day Three and Four: Recommendations

Game Times at 9:00 AM and 2:00 PM

- After your 2:00 PM game visit the National Baseball Hall of Fame and Museums or have team party and dinner. Cooperstown Concierge recommends Cooper’s Barn or Jerry’s place for your team party and dinner.

Game Times at 9:00 AM and 4:30 PM

- Following your 9:00 AM game is a good time to spend on Main Street in Cooperstown exploring all the local businesses.

Game Times at 9:00 AM and 7:00 PM

- Following your 9:00 AM game spend time on Otsego Lake. Boat rentals are available at Sam Smiths Boat Yard or canoe and kayak rentals at Brookwood Point.

Game Times at 11:30 AM and 4:30 PM

- Enjoy a team dinner at Brewery Ommegang

Game Times at 11:30 AM and 7:00 PM

- Between games enjoy time in downtown Cooperstown visiting the shops and take a ride on the Glimmerglass Queen touring Otsego lake.

Game Times at 2:00 PM and 7:00 PM

- Before your first game enjoy a trip the Fly Creek Cider Mill.

Day Five and Six: Recommendations

- Have a team party at Barnyard Swing.

- Visit the National Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum, Fenimore Farm and Country Village, Fenimore Art Museum, Fly Creek Cider Mill

- Participate in the Closing Ceremony.

Extended Stay: Recommendations

- Play a round of golf at Leatherstocking Golf Course or Otsego Golf Club

- Take a walk up to Clark Tower

- Visit the Breweries

- Enjoy time at the Hawkeye Spa

Time Recommendations for Local Attractions

Cooperstown Concierge recommends a four-hour window to visit the National Baseball Hall of Fame and Museum, allowing plenty of time for the diehard baseball fans with your team to enjoy the museum. Call ahead to discuss any potential group rates at (607) 547-7200.

We would recommend a three-hour window to visit Main Street in Cooperstown, allowing families plenty of time to explore different stores and shops the village has to offer.

We would recommend a two-hour window for any of the local restaurants or breweries in town. Feel free to call ahead to make a reservation, as most restaurants will accept large reservations.

We would recommend a four-hour window to enjoy all that The Cooperstown museums have to offer, roughly two hours for Fenimore Farm and Country Village and two hours for the Fenimore Art Museum. No reservation necessary.

Q. What are the types of accommodations in the Cooperstown Core?
A. Cooperstown offers a variety of accommodations to suit different preferences.

Hotels: There are several hotels ranging from budget-friendly options to more upscale accommodations, like the Otesaga Resort Hotel.

Bed and Breakfasts: Cooperstown has many charming B&Bs offering a cozy and personalized experience.

Vacation Rentals: You can find various vacation homes, cottages, and cabins available for short-term rental.

Q. What are somethings to consider when booking housing in Cooperstown?
A. When booking housing in Cooperstown, consider these factors:

Location: Choose a place close to Dreams Park and in the Core. Take advantage of a Core location.

Size: Ensure the property can comfortably accommodate your group.

Amenities: Look for features such as kitchens, outdoor spaces, or game rooms that cater to your group's specific needs.

Budget: Compare prices and consider any additional fees or deposits.

Reviews: Read reviews from previous guests to ensure the destination meets your expectations.

Boating & Kayaking: Rent a kayak, canoe, or paddleboard to explore the scenic beauty of Otsego Lake. There are rental options along the lakefront, and paddling around gives you a great view of the surrounding hills and wildlife. If you prefer a boat ride, consider renting a small motorboat or taking a guided tour on the lake. Swimming: There are designated swimming spots around the lake like at Glimmerglass State Park, which offers a sandy beach area. Picnicking at Glimmerglass State Park: Pack a picnic and head to Glimmerglass State Park. You can relax on the shore with great views of the lake and the surrounding landscape. It’s a lovely spot for enjoying some downtime with family or friends. Hiking: If you enjoy outdoor activities, check out some of the nearby trails. Glimmerglass State Park has easy hiking trails that offer beautiful views of the lake and surrounding forest. Fishing: Otsego Lake is known for great fishing opportunities. You can rent gear or bring your own, and fish for species like lake trout, bass, and perch. Many of the local marinas offer fishing gear for rent. Otsego Lake Boat Cruise: If you prefer a leisurely day, take a boat cruise to get a full tour of the lake and its surroundings. Some cruises are guided and provide interesting historical tidbits about the lake and Cooperstown.

The Cooperstown Beverage Trail is an excellent way to discover local beverages in the Cooperstown Area. It includes a variety of breweries, wineries, and a distillery. It’s a fun way to taste the local flavors and enjoy a day out.

The Cooperstown Core has a lot to offer beyond baseball. Here are some non-baseball activities you might enjoy:

Fenimore Art Museum: Explore American folk art, historical artifacts, and special exhibits.

Fenimore Farm and Country Village: Experience 19th-century rural life with interactive exhibits and demonstrations.

Glimmerglass State Park: Go hiking, picnicking, or enjoy nature trails with beautiful views of the lake.

Kayaking and Canoeing: Rent equipment and explore the scenic waterways around Otsego Lake.

These activities offer a great mix of culture, nature, and relaxation for a fund time in Cooperstown.

The Fly Creek Cider Mill is a delightful spot to visit. Here are some things you can do there. Sample Cider and Products: Enjoy tastings of their fresh-pressed apple cider, along with a variety of locally made products like jams, sauces, and baked goods. Explore the Mill: Take a tour of the historic mill and learn about the cider-making process. Shop for Local Goods: Browse their shop for unique gifts, gourmet foods and items made by local artisans. It’s a fun and tasty experience for all ages!

Just a mile from downtown Cooperstown, The Art Garage presents a contemporary edge to the local art scene. This gallery features exhibitions from a diverse range of artists, including outsider, emerging, and established talents. Its commitment to showcasing unconventional and thought-provoking art makes it a must-visit for those seeking a fresh perspective.

The Cooperstown Art Association is a non-profit gallery dedicated to promoting the visual arts within the community. The Cooperstown Art Association features three gallery spaces that showcase:

- Works by local and regional artists.

- Annual exhibitions, including the National Juried Art Exhibition during the summer months.

- Community events include the Spring Cleaning Art Bazaar, Fine Arts on the Lawn, and the Adorn-A-Door Wreath Festival.

The association also offers art classes, workshops, and lectures, fostering an inclusive environment for artists and art enthusiasts alike.
