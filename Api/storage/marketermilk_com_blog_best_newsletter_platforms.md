## What is an email newsletter service?

An email newsletter service is a SaaS (software as a service) solution that allows you to start and grow an email newsletter.

They say the best way to make yourself algorithm proof is to start a newsletter . It’s important to own your own list that you can tap into at any moment.

For example, it’s much more valuable to have an email list of 10,000 subscribers than it is to have 10,000 Twitter followers or YouTube subscribers.

The goal of an email newsletter service is to allow creators to capture emails, whether it’s from social media or from their website, and let them communicate directly with their audience through the medium of email. Some creators also pair their newsletter platform with an AI writer to speed up content creation.

However, not every email newsletter service is created equal.

Finding the right email newsletter platform is important. With so many newsletter services available, it can feel overwhelming to pick the right one.

Ghost is a powerful app for media creators, allowing them to publish, share, and grow their content business. It's great if you want a newsletter website that combines your blog and email list in one place. Ghost has been around since 2013, but their new refresh that’s dedicated to newsletter growth is quite intriguing (in a good way).

If you don’t already have a website, and you plan on creating a blog, Ghost is a platform you seriously want to look into. Their CMS is extremely lightweight and fast, and their newsletter platform looks promising.

They also have a minimal and clean design like beehiiv and make it easy to track all of your content metrics and analytics. You can also leverage paid subscriptions as an option to monetize your newsletter content.

### 6. Substack

If you know anything about journalism and paid newsletters, you’ve probably heard of Substack. Substack has grown in popularity in the past few years. It’s a platform that makes it super easy to start a newsletter and start getting paid (if you decide to offer a premium subscription).

Both beehiiv and MailerLite also offer paid subscription options, however what makes Substack different from other free newsletter platforms is that it's completely free to use.

The only catch is that if you do have a paid subscription option, Substack will take a 10% revenue cut from what you make. If you have a lot of subscribers, this may actually end up costing you more compared to some other Substack alternatives like beehiiv.

Regardless, Substack is a great newsletter platform if you just want to get started and get in the habit of writing to an audience. It’s really easy to search for other Substack publications, so there’s a chance that people may be able to discover your newsletter straight through Substack’s platform.

However, if you want to have your own dedicated website with a subscribe form, newsletter branding , and want to leverage features like a referral program, you should probably look into other newsletter platforms.

There’s a trend in successful Substack newsletters migrating off the platform to tools like beehiiv and MailerLite to avoid revenue fees and more control over their subscriber list.

### 7. Mailmodo

Mailmodo is a fairly new email marketing platform that allows you to create interactive emails that can make you seriously stand out from other newsletters.

This tool is a bit more technical than the ones we’ve previously mentioned, however it is a great tool if you want to create totally custom email experiences for your subscribers.

Backed by Y Combinator, the prestigious Silicon Valley venture firm, Mailmodo gives you a full WYSIWYG style editor that lets you build AMP and HTML email templates.

There are also tons of other email marketing campaign features like automations, conversion tracking, surveys and quizzes you can use your emails , and other features that help gamify your newsletter.

This truly is a great tool if you're familiar with no-code platforms and want to create a totally custom user experience for your newsletter.

Just note that there may be a slight learning curve because it is a fairly powerful tool. Setting up your newsletter may not be as intuitive as the other email newsletter tools mentioned on this list. But, Mailmodo is extremely feature-rich and there is no other newsletter platform like it on this list.

### 8. AWeber

### 9. Campaign Monitor

Similar to AWeber, Campaign Monitor is an email marketing service that allows you to collect email addresses and send newsletters.

This tool is great for small businesses that already sell products and services, but are looking to create content via a newsletter.

Campaign Monitor has an easy to use drag-and-drop email builder, along with different email templates to customize your newsletter. You can also use automation workflows to send different newsletters to different sets of subscribers.

Campaign Monitor also has tools like SMS marketing and ecommerce, which is why it’s probably best to use this tool as your newsletter platform only if you already have an existing business.

If you just plan on creating a newsletter business, you’re better off using beehiiv, MailerLite, Substack, or Revue.

### 10. Mailchimp

Mailchimp is arguably the most popular email marketing and newsletter platform. At its core, Mailchimp is a marketing CRM that is focused on email. It can be used for all types of email campaigns, newsletter’s included.

Mailchimp is quite overkill if you just want to launch a newsletter. But, because of its popularity, there are lots of resources and integrations available to make it an easy platform to use if you already have a website.

Similar to Campaign Monitor, Mailchimp is a good newsletter platform if your business is more than just a newsletter. If you already sell different products or services, Mailchimp can be a good option to use as your email marketing platform.

This is because you’ll be able to automate different parts of your email marketing strategy outside of just a newsletter. But you’ll also be able to create a newsletter on top of all the other email marketing automation stuff — hope that makes sense.

It kills two birds with one stone if you plan on utilizing email marketing for more than just a content-driven newsletter.

Mailchimp’s pricing is a standard amongst most email marketing platforms. Similar to some of the other platforms on this list, I only recommend Mailchimp if you are either just starting a newsletter as a test or if you want to leverage email marketing for other parts of your business.

For example, our main product offering is our newsletter. We originally started our newsletter with Mailchimp to validate it. Once our newsletter started growing, we decided to switch to a more newsletter-first platform like beehiiv.

If you do want to use Mailchimp because of its features, but the pricing is a bit too much, you should also check out GetResponse or Constant Contact — they are great Mailchimp alternatives.

Don’t get me wrong, Mailchimp is an amazing platform for email marketing (one of the best). But it does include a lot of features that you may not need if you just want to launch a newsletter.
