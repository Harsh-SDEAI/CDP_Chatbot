ACME CORP — EMPLOYEE BENEFITS REPORT 2026
Human Resources Department
Document Ref: HR-RPT-2026-Q1
Date: 15 March 2026
CONFIDENTIAL — For Internal Use Only
========================================


1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------
This report presents a comprehensive overview of the employee benefits programme
administered by Acme Corp during the financial year 2026. The data covers health
insurance enrollment, leave utilisation, training participation, and compensation
benchmarks across all four regional offices.

Key findings indicate a 12% increase in health plan uptake and a reduction in
average sick-leave days from 6.4 to 5.1 per employee per annum. Detailed
breakdowns are provided in the tables and sections below.

The HR department recommends a renewed focus on study leave awareness and
an expansion of the mental health support programme based on the findings
in section 6 of this document.


2. LEAVE ENTITLEMENT POLICY
----------------------------
All permanent employees are entitled to paid leave as outlined in company
policy document Ref: HR-POL-2024-03. Leave accrues at a rate of 1.75 days
per month of continuous service.

Table 1: Leave Entitlements by Employment Grade (FY 2026)
----------------------------------------------------------
| Grade                | Annual Leave | Sick Leave | Maternity/Paternity | Study Leave |
|----------------------|-------------|------------|---------------------|-------------|
| Grade 1 — Junior     | 15 days     | 10 days    | 12 weeks            | 5 days      |
| Grade 2 — Mid        | 18 days     | 10 days    | 14 weeks            | 8 days      |
| Grade 3 — Senior     | 22 days     | 12 days    | 16 weeks            | 10 days     |
| Grade 4 — Manager    | 25 days     | 14 days    | 18 weeks            | 12 days     |
| Grade 5 — Director   | 30 days     | 14 days    | 20 weeks            | 15 days     |
----------------------------------------------------------
Caption: All values represent calendar days unless stated otherwise.
         Maternity/Paternity leave is fully paid for all grades.


3. HEALTH INSURANCE ENROLLMENT SUMMARY
----------------------------------------
The table below shows health plan enrollment statistics across all four offices
for the period January to March 2026.

Table 2: Health Insurance Enrollment by Office — Q1 2026
---------------------------------------------------------
| Office       | Total Staff | Enrolled | Enrollment % | Avg Monthly Premium |
|--------------|-------------|----------|--------------|---------------------|
| Mumbai HQ    | 320         | 298      | 93.1%        | Rs. 4,200           |
| Delhi        | 185         | 171      | 92.4%        | Rs. 4,050           |
| Bangalore    | 210         | 204      | 97.1%        | Rs. 4,350           |
| Pune         | 95          | 88       | 92.6%        | Rs. 3,980           |
| TOTAL        | 810         | 761      | 93.9%        | Rs. 4,160 avg       |
---------------------------------------------------------
Caption: Premium figures are employer-subsidised rates.
         Bangalore leads enrollment at 97.1%, up from 94.2% in Q4 2025.


4. TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT
-----------------------------
Training participation data for Q1 2026 is summarised below.

Table 3: Training Hours by Department — Q1 2026
-------------------------------------------------
| Department         | Employees | Avg Hours/Person | Total Hours | Budget Used |
|--------------------|-----------|-----------------|-------------|-------------|
| Engineering        | 142       | 18.4            | 2,613       | 74%         |
| Sales              | 98        | 22.1            | 2,166       | 88%         |
| Finance            | 54        | 14.2            | 767         | 61%         |
| Human Resources    | 38        | 16.8            | 638         | 69%         |
| Operations         | 120       | 12.5            | 1,500       | 55%         |
| Marketing          | 67        | 19.3            | 1,293       | 79%         |
| TOTAL              | 519       | 17.3            | 8,977       | 72%         |
-------------------------------------------------
Caption: Budget utilisation calculated against Q1 2026 L&D allocation.
         Sales department highest utilisation at 88%.


5. KEY OBSERVATIONS
---------------------
The following key points were identified during the review period:

  - Bangalore office leads enrollment at 97.1%, up from 94.2% in Q4 2025.
  - Average sick leave per employee declined by 20% year-on-year.
  - Maternity and paternity uptake increased by 34% following the revised policy.
  - Study leave utilisation remains low at 38% of entitled days.
  - Grade 5 Directors reported the highest leave balance carryover (avg 8.2 days).
  - Engineering department recorded the highest absenteeism rate at 3.2%.
  - Overall benefits satisfaction score improved from 6.8 to 7.4 out of 10.


6. MENTAL HEALTH AND WELLNESS
-------------------------------
The Employee Assistance Programme (EAP) was utilised by 12.4% of the workforce
in Q1 2026, up from 9.1% in the same period last year. This increase is considered
positive as it reflects growing awareness rather than declining wellbeing.

Sessions breakdown:
  - Counselling sessions:       214
  - Financial advice sessions:   88
  - Legal advice sessions:       42
  - Career coaching sessions:    67
  - Total sessions:             411

The average number of sessions per user was 3.2. Confidentiality of all
EAP interactions is maintained as per policy HR-POL-2023-07.


7. COMPENSATION BENCHMARKING
------------------------------
Acme Corp commissioned an external salary benchmarking study in February 2026.
Results indicate the following positioning against market median:

Table 4: Compensation vs Market Median by Grade
-------------------------------------------------
| Grade              | Acme Median Salary | Market Median | Position vs Market |
|--------------------|-------------------|---------------|--------------------|
| Grade 1 — Junior   | Rs. 4,20,000      | Rs. 4,05,000  | +3.7% above        |
| Grade 2 — Mid      | Rs. 6,80,000      | Rs. 6,50,000  | +4.6% above        |
| Grade 3 — Senior   | Rs. 10,50,000     | Rs. 10,80,000 | -2.8% below        |
| Grade 4 — Manager  | Rs. 16,20,000     | Rs. 15,90,000 | +1.9% above        |
| Grade 5 — Director | Rs. 28,00,000     | Rs. 27,50,000 | +1.8% above        |
-------------------------------------------------
Caption: Salary figures are annual CTC (Cost to Company) in Indian Rupees.
         Grade 3 Senior roles are below market — salary review recommended.


8. RECOMMENDATIONS
--------------------
Based on the findings of this report, the HR department recommends the following
actions for Q2 2026:

  1. Launch a study leave awareness campaign targeting all grades, with a focus
     on Grade 1 and Grade 2 employees who have the lowest utilisation rates.

  2. Conduct a salary review for Grade 3 Senior employees to address the 2.8%
     gap identified in the benchmarking study.

  3. Expand the Employee Assistance Programme to include dedicated financial
     planning workshops given the high demand for financial advice sessions.

  4. Introduce a wellness day policy allowing each employee one additional paid
     day per quarter for mental health and personal wellbeing activities.

  5. Set a target of 95% health insurance enrollment across all offices by
     the end of Q2 2026, with specific focus on Delhi and Pune offices.
