CDP Shop: www.cdpshop.com

CDP Shop offers a simple shopping experience with categories for Mens, Womens, and Youth, covering baseball pants, shirts, shorts, socks, and accessories. It has a dedicated section for team baseball pants and tournament shirts, along with customization options like embroidery and engraving to personalize gear.
CDP Shop also features a wide souvenir collection including pins, caps, bags, frames, and jewelry, plus access to official Dreams Park game videos and photos. Users can explore featured products for gift ideas, use tools like Wishlist and Cart, and create an account for easier shopping. Support pages for contact.


CDP Photo App: www.dreamsparkphotos.com

Dreams Park Photos is the official photo platform of Cooperstown Dreams Park, where families can browse, download, and order professional photos from their player's tournament. Photos are sorted into easy categories like Studio, Action & Game, Community, and Umpire shots.
Users can download digital copies or order standard prints (4x6, 5x7, 8x10), and also create fun keepsakes like posters, collages, magazine covers, baseball cards, and photo pin or magnet flags, with new themed templates available too. There's also a Wish List, search tool, Photos of the Week, and easy support options, making it simple to turn Cooperstown memories into lasting photo products.
