COOPERSTOWN BASEBALL ["Cooperstown: Where Baseball Legends Live On"]  

There is no town in the United States that better embodies the game of baseball than Cooperstown, New York. The feeling of being surrounded by the energy of past and current players is extraordinary. Taking a trip down Main Street gives visitors a nostalgic view into baseball's history that excites both young and old. The Village of Cooperstown adds even more magic to the experience of the baseball tournaments at Cooperstown Dreams Park. Participants of Dreams Park will have the opportunity to visit the National Baseball Hall of Fame, and attend baseball games at Doubleday Field, the legendary home of baseball, where the game began one afternoon in 1839. There are approximately 500,000 visitors who travel every year to the enchanting Leatherstocking Region for their own piece of the dream. These true fans come to watch ball games at Doubleday Field and walk the streets in awe of the heroes who walked before them. Now, they come to Cooperstown Dreams Park. Playing baseball at Cooperstown Dreams Park is an experience that lasts a lifetime.
