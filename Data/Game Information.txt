GAME INFORMATION
All visitors MUST be affiliated with a team to enter the park.

GAME SCHEDULE:
Will be available 1-2 days prior to arrival on the website/App.
Teams are guaranteed 5 pool play games. (WEATHER PERMITTING)
Tournament play is single elimination. Each team is guaranteed 1 tournament game (WEATHER PERMITTING)

WEATHER/FIELDS:
We try to get all games in, even if it is playing later into the night, weather permitting, for completion.There are 22 Lighted Fields with Webcasting abilities. Chairs with backs are available on fields 1-2 and 4-22.  Bleachers are available at Little Majors Stadium.  Spectators can bring their own chairs if they want.
