Team Umpires:
-One Umpire, selected from Cooperstown Dreams Park’s Umpire Program or referred by the Team Contact, is required per team.
-Team Contact must log on to their dashboard to either indicate their team is looking for an umpire and then select one, or to refer an umpire.

Teams Attending & Game Schedules will be available on the Cooperstown Dreams Park App and website approximately 1-2 days before the start of each tournament.
