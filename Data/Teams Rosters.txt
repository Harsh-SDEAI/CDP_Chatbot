TEAMS/ROSTERS
Questions not answered by information below, refer to https://www.cooperstowndreamspark.com/
Players: 
Age/ID Requirements:
ID Required:
-Original Birth Certificate or Certified Copy with raised seal
-Valid Passport/Nexus Card
-Military/ State Issued ID
Player ID’s are handed in at registration, and returned to the coach at checkout.

Rosters:
-All teams must have AT LEAST 11 players and 2 coaches. NO exceptions.
-All persons on the roster, listed as coaches, must complete coach resume in the Coach Dashboard prior to check-in day.
-All persons in clubhouse (Max 20), in dugout and on the field MUST be on the official roster and paid for.
-Scorekeeper (if not on roster) must sit in parent section. HOME team is the official book and operates the scoreboard. An official score sheet will be provided to hand in to the Baseball Operations Team at the end of each game.
Rosters should be entered by the team contact via the Team Contact dashboard in the “My Team” Section of the website before the April 1 deadline. 
-Teams can only make changes to the roster themselves up to one month prior to arrival, after that they must email baseball operations for any changes. baseballoperations@cooperstowndreamspark.com 
-Include in your email:
-Date playing
-Team Name and Number
-Specific Changes needed/All information
