BASEBALL ACADEMY
Cooperstown Dreams Park is currently developing a weekly, alumni based program to cultivate an appreciation for the fundamentals of baseball both on and off the field. The vision of the program is to incorporate alumni, 11U, and 10U boys and girls who love the game. Through a regimen of on the field training and skill development, the Baseball Academy and Student of the Game program will expose participants to a tournament setting as well as some of the best resources, coaches, trainers, technology and equipment available. Off the field, through an established curriculum, these programs will focus on developing a greater appreciation for all aspects of the game, including history, community, tradition, and preservation of values.
