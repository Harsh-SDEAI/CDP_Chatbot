CHECK-IN
Check-in:	
Team registration is completed by the team contact and/or coach with all player identifications together for the entire team.
All Coaches must check in at the “Coach Check-in” table in the Registration Pavilion, with their ID, to receive their Coach Wristband.
All Players & Parents can come into the park between 2:30 PM and 8 PM and go straight to their clubhouse. Clubhouse assignment will be available on the App, or by asking the Guest Service personnel at the North/West Gate upon walking into the village.
Parents can remain in the village 2:30 PM- 8 PM on Day One.
Parents can only re-enter the village from 6 AM- 9 AM on Day Seven.  
If a team checks out before Day 7, Parents are not able to enter.
