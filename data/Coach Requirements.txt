Coach Requirements: 

Coach Participation Waivers are now electronic on the Coach dashboard.

	1.Coaches will need to obtain their individual security code from the Team Contact and will be able to sign into the dashboard once the coach is placed on the Team Roster. This form will automatically pop up for them to fill out.

	2.Coach Resumes are now electronic on the Coach dashboard. This form will automatically pop up for them to fill out. This is a requirement to coach.

Once completed online the “Background Consent” checkmark will appear on the roster. The “Resume” box will not be checked until the background check has been completed on our end.
All coaches must show photo ID to receive their coaching wristband. (NOT kept for the week).
All coaches over 18 must complete medical questionnaire and upload an immunization record, and insurance card to the Coach dashboard (If no immunization record/insurance card available must upload a piece of paper with their Name, Date of Birth and signature stating such).
Coaches under the age of 18 must upload medical information signed by Dr. (same as players). We DO NOT keep their ID for the week.
