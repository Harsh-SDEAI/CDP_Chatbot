
Welcome to Cooperstown Dreams Park – your gateway to an unforgettable youth baseball experience!

Playing in the prestigious American Youth Baseball Hall of Fame Invitational Tournament at Cooperstown Dreams Park is a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity for your baseball community. To register your team for the summer season, simply visit the “Register” page on the Dreams Park website, complete the form, and send it in. If you have any questions, contact Baseball Operations directly.

Age eligibility is clearly defined: players must not turn 13 before May 1st of the year they are participating.

Before arriving at Cooperstown Dreams Park, all registered teams, families, and friends are encouraged to download the Cooperstown Dreams Park app and register. This app grants facility access and provides helpful visit information. Admission is free for all guests, and please note that CDP operates as a drive-in and drive-out facility only.

Parking is free of charge, with multiple lots available for convenient access to baseball fields, the Retail Center, and other amenities. All vehicles must be registered at the Front Gate. Please be advised that RVs and campers are not permitted within the park.

Cooperstown Dreams Park features 22 world-class, professionally manicured grass fields with no time limits for games. Spectator areas are well-equipped with chairs with backs at most fields, except for Little Majors Stadium, which has bleachers. Visitors are welcome to bring their own chairs as well.

For cash needs, there are two ATMs available: one located inside the Baseball Village near the Village Office, and the other at the Family Pavilion.

Regarding bag policies, all registered players, coaches, umpires, and guests may bring bags into the facility. However, all bags are subject to inspection upon entry.

Food and drink policies are aligned with New York State Department of Health regulations. Outside food and beverages are not permitted, except for bottled water. Alcoholic beverages are strictly prohibited at all times. For detailed guidelines, please consult the Rules of Dreams Park available on their website.

Photography is welcome for personal use. While all games are live-streamed and recorded, guests may also record games from within spectator sections. No recording equipment is allowed on the field itself, and drones or other unmanned aircraft are strictly forbidden.

For team check-in, coaches and parents/players must ensure all checklist items are completed before arrival. The Team Contact is responsible for submitting Official Birth Certificates at registration. Relevant forms and additional instructions can be accessed via your team dashboard. For any clarifications, reach out to the Baseball Operations team.

We look forward to welcoming you to Cooperstown Dreams Park!
