DIRECTIONS
Cooperstown Dreams Park is located approximately four miles south of Cooperstown, New York. We are located approximately 70 miles west of Albany, 30 miles south of New York State Thruway (I-90), 13 miles north of I-88, and 60 miles east of Binghamton.

Please Note: In 2001 New York State became the first state in the nation to ban the use of a handheld cell phone while driving a motor vehicle.

If you would like the physical address of Cooperstown Dreams Park for GPS purposes please use the following:

Address: 4550 state highway 28 Milford, New York 13807

____________________________________________________________________________________________

FROM ALBANY AND BOSTON
Use New York state thruway (I-90) exit 25a, take I-88 west to exit 17 to route 28 north for 13 miles and hartwick seminary.
Use New York state thruway (I-90) exit 29 at canajoharie, take routes 10, 20, and 80 into Cooperstown. Cooperstown Dreams Park is located four miles south of Cooperstown on route 28 south in hartwick seminary.

FROM NEW YORK CITY
Use interstate 87 north until reaching interstate 90 west. Take New York state thruway (I-90) exit 25a, then take I-88 west to exit 17 to route 28 north for 13 miles and hartwick seminary.

FROM SYRACUSE AND BUFFALO
Use New York state thruway (I-90) exit 30 at herkimer and take route 28 south to Cooperstown. Cooperstown Dreams Park is located four miles south of Cooperstown on route 28 south in hartwick seminary.

FROM BINGHAMTON
Use I-88 east to exit 17, go north on route 28 and proceed 13 miles to hartwick seminary.
