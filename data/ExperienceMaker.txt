ExperienceMaker 
Photo/Video Pass
The ExperienceMaker package can be opted out of by emailing baseballoperations@cooperstowndreamspark.com. Purchase is non-refundable.  
If not chosen: The team has the option to purchase photos and videos individually if they opt to not add the ExperienceMaker. (No free download & no free baseball cards if opt out)

The ExperienceMaker code gives access, for each team member, to:
-Digital downloads for all game photos, community photos, team photo and baseball card (Photographers are at all day games)
-Live streaming of all games, ceremonies and skills competitions
-Digital downloads of streamed games
-Digital download of studio session player video
-Prints of team photo and baseball cards delivered onsite. 
-Each ExperienceMaker holder also receives 5 free additional “community access codes” for family and friends to dreamsparkTV towatch livestreaming games and ceremonies.



Steps to Download Digital Images. (For ExperienceMaker Users)

1. Visit https://dreamsparkphotos.com and log in with your credentials.

2. After logging in successfully, you will be redirected to the Home page.

3. Start by adding images to your Wish List.

       Browse categories such as Studio, Action & Game Photos, Community, and Umpire.

       To add a photo, find the desired image and click the Wish List icon.

3. To view your selected images, click the Wish List icon located in the top-right corner of the header.

4. Within the Wish List, each image will display one of the following statuses:

    Processing Your Image: The image is currently being prepared in high resolution.

    Ready for Download: The image is fully processed. Click the Download icon to save it to your device.

    Already Downloaded: Indicates that the image has already been downloaded.

5. When an image shows Ready for Download, click the Download icon to save it to your device.

6. You can also view your download history, including the date and the number of times each image was   downloaded, in the My Downloaded Files section.




PHOTOGRAPHY INFORMATION
GAMES:
Our Photography staff (weather permitting) photograph the first 3 game sets Days 3-5 (9:00am, 11:30am, 2:00pm) and 3 sets Day 6 (9:00am, 12:00pm, and 3:00pm). We do not photograph games after 6:00pm due to lighting. Once the last game of the set is over it may take 2-3 hours for your game to appear on the website.

REQUESTS:
We offer the opportunity for our guests to make photo requests; they are not a guarantee. We suggest waiting until after your first game before making photo requests to see what images may have been taken. However, if you wish to make one before your first game we will not turn them away. Photo requests can be made in our ExperienceMaker Customization Studio which is under the Family Pavilion. If you did not get the chance to put in a photo request before your game you can call the photographer over and ask them while they are at your game.
