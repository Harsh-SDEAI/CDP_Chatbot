FOOD INFORMATION
OUTSIDE FOOD/DRINK:
NO outside food and drink allowed to be brought into the Park, for health & safety.
Teams can have plastic bottles of water in clubhouses.
Teams can have parents bring water/ice and drop it for coach pick up at the West and/or North gates.
-Bagged ice is sold in the Snack Shacks.
-Snack Shacks (See department Schedules for open times) All major credit cards/ apple pay/ cash accepted.

TEAM MEALS:
Teams are provided Breakfast, Lunch and Dinner daily; Meal times are posted in the Dining Pavilion inside Baseball Village. Meal times are firm unless specifically extended due to weather delay. Meal time extensions will be noted on any updated schedule published due to weather delays. Teams should make arrangements to eat before any game that might run later than meal times allow.

FOOD/ALLERGIES:
ALL Allergy & Special Diet request should be submitted on the completed medical forms- uploaded to Parent/Player dashboard.
