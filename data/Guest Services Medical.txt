GUEST SERVICES/MEDICAL

FIRSTAID/MEDICAL:
Provided 24 hours a day.
ALL medical questions should be sent to healthdirector@cooperstowndreamspark.com 
•Include:
	1. Date playing
	2.Team Name and Number
	3.Player name
	4.Question / Issue
Physical exams completed within a year are ok, as long as they have all information asked for on the official Dreams Park form. Attach outside physical along with the Dreams Park Camper Medication form.
All medication MUST be stored at the infirmary and dispensed by medical staff
Exception: Diabetic testing supplies, Asthma inhalers and Epi-Pens.
