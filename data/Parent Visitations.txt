
PARENT VISITATIONS:
Parents are ONLY allowed in the village between 2:30 PM and 8 PM Day One (check-in time), and 6 AM-9 AM Day Seven (official check-out time).

PLAYER CHECK-OUT:
Players are allowed to spend time with their families outside of the park. Players can be signed out using a blue check out form.  
-All PLAYERS must have a check out form. 
-Two signatures are required- parent and coach.
-Check out forms can be found in the Village Office, at all gates to the Player Village, and the Baseball Operations Center in Family Pavilion.
Parents may eat with their players at Snack Shacks and/or in the Family Pavilion. Village Dining Pavilion is for TEAMS only.
-NO Pets allowed on property- Unless a Service Animal has been approved by Guest Services. Email request to  info@cooperstowndreamspark.com 
-NO MOTOR HOMES (RVs) allowed in the park- NO EXCEPTIONS.
-CDP is a drive-in and drive-out facility ONLY.
For family accommodation questions refer to the Family Guide located on our website: https://www.cooperstowndreamspark.com/FamilyGuide


HANDICAPPED ACCESSABILITY:
-We are fully handicap accessible with handicap parking and have transport carts available to assist with bringing our guests to and from their destinations within the facility. Handicap Scooters are permitted, with approval. Email request to  info@cooperstowndreamspark.com
