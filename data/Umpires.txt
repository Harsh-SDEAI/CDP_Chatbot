Umpires
Umpire Requirements:
-Must be 18 or older.
-Belong to an umpire association or assigner in their hometown.
-Complete and submit one CDP Umpire Program Form (on website)
-Once Umpire Program form is processed must log into their Umpire dashboard and complete Waiver and Acknowledgments, Background and Character check and then must indicate availability.

Umpires Package Received:
-2 Cooperstown Dreams Park Pro-Style Umpire Jersey
-T-Shirt
-Combo Hat
-Pullover 
-Water Bottle
-Plate Brush
-Ball Bag
-15 trading Pins

Umpires Must Provide Own:
-Heather Grey or Medium Grey Pants 
-Umpire Jerseys
-Shoes
-Protective Gear

Needed for Check-In:
-Photo ID and Certificate of Participation to receive Umpire wristband.
-Will receive a Certificate of Participation for each tournament session through the Umpire Dashboard and will be required to present it at registration.

Umpire Clubhouses:	Communal- each Umpire gets an entire bunk, top for belongings, bottom for sleeping.

Umpire Bathhouse: 	Bathhouse G is reserved for Umpires/Employees

UNIFORM CLEANING SERVICES: Cleaning service is provided for each Umpire for uniforms ONLY.  Sign-up is required with Uniform Cleaning Services.
