UNIFORMS/EQUIPMENT

UNIFORMS/EQUIPMENT:
PLAYERS RECEIVE:
-1 Home & 1 Away Jersey
-2 pair of socks- 1 Navy- 1 Scarlet
-Game Hat
-Short Sleeved Performance Tee 
-Long Sleeve Performance Tee
-Batting Practice Jacket

PLAYERS MUST PROVIDE:
-Solid white knickers-style baseball pants (NO pinstripes, piping or logos allowed)
-These can be purchased on the website- 2 pairs of pants and 2 belts for $56. (11 or more ordered together price drops to 50$ each set)
-https://www.cdpshop.com/team-baseball-pants.html 
-Belts:  Belts must be Scarlet and Navy 
-Any color shoes: No metal cleats, rubber cleats only. 

COACHES RECEIVE:
-Game Hat
-Pullover Jacket
-Batting Practice Jacket
-1 Home & 1 Away Uniform Polo Shirt

COACHES MUST PROVIDE: Navy or Khaki shorts or pants for game uniform, NO JEANS.

TEAMS RECEIVE: Team Backpack, Ball Bucket, Scorebook, Lineup Cards

TEAMS MUST PROVIDE:
-Helmets
-Practice balls (CDP will provide game balls only)
-Bats:  No wooden bats, no modified bats (See USSSA list for banned bats)
-Catcher’s Gear
-Gloves
-Protective Gear
-Slider shorts
-Team Wagon

Uniform numbers are issued 1-15 at random. The coach will dictate uniform number distribution. 

Banners: No larger than 3’ X 6’.

Pins: Recommend 100-110 per player. Daily pin trading events scheduled: See App

UNIFORM CLEANING SERVICES:
Cleaning service is provided for each team for uniforms only.  
-The system is explained to each coach at check-in.
-Label all uniforms on the tag with CLUBHOUSE NUMBER.
-Mesh bags provided for cleaning services MUST be returned at check-out or the coach will be responsible for purchasing new bags at $4 each.
